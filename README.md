# CareLoop

An AI voice agent that automatically calls patients after discharge or a visit to check on their recovery, answer basic questions, and help them book a follow-up — all without staff having to make manual calls.

## The Problem

After a patient leaves a clinic or hospital, there's usually no easy way to track how they're doing. Follow-ups get missed, complications go unnoticed until it's too late, and rebooking a visit means the patient has to call back themselves.

## How It Works

A patient visits a clinic (say, dermatology) and is told to report back after a week. Instead of relying on the patient to remember, our AI agent calls them automatically, asks how they're doing, listens for any issues, and logs everything into a report for the doctor.

## Features

- **Automatic Follow-Up Calls** — Calls patients at the right time after their visit or discharge, no manual scheduling needed.
- **Natural Conversation** — Asks about symptoms, side effects, pain, or general recovery in a friendly, human-like way.
- **Smart Alerts** — If a patient reports something serious or concerning, the clinic is notified right away instead of waiting.
- **Instant Reports** — Every call is turned into a clean, easy-to-read summary for the doctor or clinic staff.
- **In-Call Booking** — If the patient wants to see the doctor again, they can book (or reschedule) their appointment during the same call.
- **Missed Call Follow-Up** — If a patient doesn't pick up, the agent retries and can send a WhatsApp/SMS link as backup.
- **Medicine Reminders** — Gently checks if patients are taking their prescribed medication and flags if they aren't.
- **Multilingual** — Works in 10+ Indian languages plus English, so language is never a barrier to good care.
- **Dashboard for Clinics** — A simple view for hospital staff to see all patient updates and flagged cases at a glance.

## Why It Matters

- Patients get consistent care without extra effort.
- Clinics catch problems early instead of after they worsen.
- Staff save time since calls, notes, and reports happen automatically.
- More patients return for follow-ups since booking is effortless.
